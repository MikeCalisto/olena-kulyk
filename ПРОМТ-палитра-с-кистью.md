# Палитра: заменить мастихин на кисть

Два варианта. Первый лучше — правка существующей картинки, всё остальное
гарантированно останется тем же. Второй на случай, если правка испортит палитру.

---

## Вариант 1 (основной) — правка существующего файла

Загрузи в ChatGPT исходник `ChatGPT Image 5 сент. 2026 г., 11_26_16.png`
(я приложил его к сообщению) и дай такой промт:

```
Edit this image: replace the metal palette knife with an artist paint brush,
lying in exactly the same position and at the same diagonal angle across the
palette. The brush has a pale natural wood handle, a polished silver ferrule,
and soft natural bristles with a little wet teal-blue oil paint loaded on the
tip. Keep the brush the same overall length and visual weight as the knife it
replaces.

Change nothing else. Keep the wooden oval palette exactly as it is — same shape,
same thumb hole, same wood grain, same worn edges. Keep every dab of oil paint
exactly where it is: the white, the gold ochre, the silver grey, the deep teal,
the rust brown, the cream, with the same impasto peaks and the same smears on
the wood. Keep the same camera angle, the same lighting from the top-left, the
same shadows, the same transparent background with a clean hard alpha edge and
no colored fringe.
```

## Вариант 2 (запасной) — генерация с нуля

Если правка поплывёт — сгенерируй заново по полному описанию:

```
An oval wooden artist palette seen at a three-quarter angle, tilted and floating
in mid-air. Pale warm wood with visible grain, a thumb hole on the right side,
edges worn from use. On the palette: six thick glossy dabs of oil paint —
titanium white, warm gold ochre, silver grey, deep teal-blue, rust brown and
cream — each holding heavy impasto peaks, with dried smears of the same colours
worked into the wood around them.

An artist paint brush lies diagonally across the palette: pale natural wood
handle, polished silver ferrule, soft natural bristles with wet teal-blue paint
on the tip.

Transparent background PNG, isolated object floating in mid-air, no ground, no
cast shadow behind it. Clean hard alpha edge, no colored fringe, no glow, no
halo. Soft studio lighting from top-left, subtle rim light. Photorealistic 3D
product render, sharp focus, high detail, centered, square 1:1, empty space
around the object. No text, no logo, no watermark, no hands, no people.
```
